Campus & Facilities

An 8,014 m² campus with 26 classrooms, 6 laboratories, a library, playgrounds and hostel — designed for safe, hands-on learning.

{{icon:flask}}
Laboratories

Science labs — learning by doing

Separate, well-equipped Physics, Chemistry and Biology laboratories let students perform the experiments they read about — from titrations to microscope slides. Practical work is part of the timetable from the middle classes upward and essential for Classes XI and XII.

  • Physics lab
  • Chemistry lab
  • Biology lab
  • Mathematics lab
{{icon:book}}
Library

A library that builds readers

Our well-stocked library offers textbooks, reference books, story books, encyclopaedias, newspapers and magazines in English and Hindi. A quiet, comfortable reading room and a dedicated library period encourage the reading habit from an early age.

  • Reference & competitive books
  • Story books for juniors
  • Daily newspapers
  • Library period every week
