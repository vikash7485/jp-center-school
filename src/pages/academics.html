Academics

CBSE curriculum, delivered by caring teachers — from the first steps in Nursery to the Class XII board examinations.

Our Approach

Learning that is joyful, rigorous and practical

We follow the CBSE curriculum in line with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), with a focus on concept clarity, experiential learning and regular, transparent assessment.

Nursery · LKG · UKG

Pre-Primary (Foundational Stage)

Children learn through play, stories, songs and hands-on activities in bright, safe classrooms.

  • Phonics, early reading and writing (English & Hindi)
  • Number sense, shapes, colours and patterns
  • Rhymes, art & craft, clay modelling, music and movement
  • Fine and gross motor skills, good habits and social skills
Class I – V

Primary (Preparatory Stage)

Strong foundations in literacy and numeracy with activity-based, joyful teaching.

  • English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS
  • Computer basics, General Knowledge, Art and Music
  • Value education, storytelling and show & tell
  • Physical education, yoga and games every week
Class VI – X

Middle & Secondary

Deeper conceptual understanding, lab work and step-by-step preparation for the Class X CBSE board.

  • English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Science
  • Computer Science / Information Technology
  • Regular lab practicals, projects and periodic tests
  • Board-exam practice, sample papers and doubt-clearing sessions
Class XI – XII

Senior Secondary

Focused streams with expert PGT faculty, practicals and career guidance.

  • Science (PCM): Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English + optional
  • Science (PCB): Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English + optional
  • Commerce: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English + optional
  • Guidance for NEET, JEE, CUET and other competitive exams
Assessment

Regular, fair and transparent evaluation

Our assessment system follows CBSE guidelines and helps every child — and every parent — know exactly where they stand.

  • Periodic tests and class assessments through the year
  • Half-yearly and session-ending (annual) examinations
  • Projects, practicals, portfolios and subject enrichment activities
  • Report cards and Parent-Teacher Meetings after every term
  • Remedial classes and extra support for students who need it
