Welcome to JPCS

A school where every child is seen, guided and encouraged to grow.

At J.P. Central School our chief objective is to educate children in an environment that respects their aptitudes and interests, and gives them every opportunity to discover and demonstrate their natural abilities.

We pair a strong CBSE academic programme with sports, arts, values and life skills, so our students grow into confident, responsible young people ready for a fast-changing, technology-driven world.

  • CBSE curriculum, English medium
  • Experienced, trained faculty
  • Science, computer & maths labs
  • Well-stocked library
  • Sports, yoga & athletics
  • Hostel facility available
