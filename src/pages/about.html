Our Story

Quality education for the children of Samastipur

J.P. Central School was established in {{school.established}} at Mathurapur Ghat, Samastipur, with a simple promise: to give every child a high-quality education that develops academic excellence, character and holistic growth.

Today we are a co-educational, English-medium school affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi (Affiliation No. {{school.affiliation}}, School Code {{school.schoolCode}}), teaching students from Nursery to Class XII, with Science and Commerce streams at the senior secondary level.

We believe every child can succeed. Our experienced teachers, safe and nurturing campus, labs, library and sports facilities, and a wide range of co-curricular activities help each student discover their strengths and reach their full potential.

We value diversity, inclusivity and community. We welcome students from all walks of life, celebrate their unique backgrounds, and encourage them to become responsible and ethical citizens who contribute positively to society.

Director's Message

Shaping responsible citizens and leaders

As Director of this school, my primary goal is to create an environment that fosters learning, growth and development for all of our students. Education is not just about imparting knowledge and information — it is about shaping individuals who will become responsible citizens and leaders in their communities.

To achieve this, we have built a culture of inclusivity, where every student is valued for who they are and what they bring. I encourage our teachers to use a range of teaching methods that suit different learning styles, and to foster critical thinking, creativity and curiosity in the classroom.

I also believe in strong relationships with parents, families and the wider community. We encourage open communication between the school and the families we serve, and we are committed to a safe and nurturing environment that supports the social, emotional and physical wellbeing of every child.

Finally, we keep ourselves up to date with the latest developments in education, and continually improve our programmes, so that J.P. Central School remains a dynamic institution providing high-quality education to all.

Mahesh Kumar
Director, J.P. Central School
Principal's Message

Together we will achieve great things

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to J.P. Central School. Our school is dedicated to providing a high-quality education that promotes academic excellence, character development and the holistic growth of every student.

Our community has always come together with resilience, determination and compassion. Building on that, we continue to offer a rigorous academic programme that prepares our students for success in board examinations, college and beyond — while keeping the health, safety and happiness of our children at the centre of everything we do.

We place great importance on teacher training, so that our faculty stay up to date with the best teaching practices. Beyond academics, we encourage every child to take part in sports and co-curricular activities that build leadership, team spirit and a sense of responsibility.

I thank our students, parents and teachers for their dedication and hard work. I am confident that together, we will achieve great things.

Qamar Azam
Principal · M.A., B.Ed.
