Admissions 2026-27

Admissions are open for Nursery to Class IX, and for Class XI (Session 2026-28) in Science and Commerce. Seats are limited.

How to Apply

Admission process

A simple, fair and transparent process — our office team will guide you at every step.

Registration

Collect the admission form from the school office or register online. Fill in the child's and parents' details accurately.

Submit Documents

Submit the completed form along with the required documents (see the list below).

Assessment

For Class I and above, a simple entrance assessment in English, Mathematics and Science (as applicable) to understand the child's level.

Interaction

A friendly interaction with the child and parents to understand interests, needs and expectations — and to answer your questions.

Confirmation & Fee

On selection, complete enrolment within the given time by submitting original documents and paying the admission fee and first instalment.

Admission Enquiry

Request a call back

Fill in the form — it will open WhatsApp with your details ready to send to our admission desk.

Or call directly: +91 94304 41288 · +91 98018 38725

Documents

Documents required

  • Birth certificate (proof of age)
  • Aadhaar card of the student and parents
  • Report card of the previous class (Class I onwards)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous school, if applicable
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs of the child, 2 of each parent
  • Address proof (electricity bill / ration card / rent agreement)
  • Caste / category certificate, if applicable
  • Class X marksheet & migration certificate (for Class XI, other boards)
